Delete your account

Last updated: October 16, 2026 · Applies to the Job Profit Guard beta

In the app

Owners: open Account → Delete account and data. Your business and everything in it — jobs, check-ins, change orders, photos, receipts, reports and conversations — are deleted immediately, and your crew lose access.

Crew: open Account → Delete my account. Your account is deleted immediately; days you filed stay in your boss's records without your name.

Without the app

Email support@jobprofitguard.app from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm it is you, then delete the account and its data within 30 days.

Want a copy first? In the app, Account → Export your data saves spreadsheets or a full backup.

What is kept

Nothing that belongs to the deleted account, with two exceptions: days a crew member filed stay in their boss's job records without their name, and our nightly database backups are kept for 14 days, so deleted data is gone from them within 14 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
