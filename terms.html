What Job Profit Guard is

Job Profit Guard is a job-management and decision-support tool for contractors. It tracks your jobs, costs, and progress, and it surfaces projections and suggestions to help you protect your margin.

What it is not

Profit projections, risk flags, AI-detected extra work, scope analyses, and generated change orders may be incomplete or wrong. They are not legal, tax, accounting, or contractual advice. Figures the app projects are estimates built from the records you enter — they are not a promise about how a job will turn out.

Your responsibilities

You remain responsible for checking every figure before relying on it, obtaining your customer's approval before performing extra work, the accuracy of what you enter, and every business decision you make.
