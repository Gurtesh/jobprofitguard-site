What Job Profit Guard is

Job Profit Guard is a job-management and decision-support tool for contractors. It tracks your jobs, costs and progress, and it surfaces projections, warnings and suggestions to help you protect your margin.

Figures the app projects are estimates built from the records you enter — not a promise about how a job will turn out.
