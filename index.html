Job Profit Guard

Your job can look busy and still lose money.

Job Profit Guard connects the original plan, today's costs, and customer changes into one owner-first view — a 30-second daily check-in that catches profit leaks before the job is finished.

In the app: voice check-ins that become reviewed numbers, receipt scanning with printed subtotal / tax / total, change orders with customer-ready PDFs, and Frank — a foreman who reads your records and never changes them.
