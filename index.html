Installing it

  1. Tap Download for Android, then open the file when it finishes.
  2. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect says it doesn't recognise the app, tap More details → Install anyway — that's normal for apps installed from outside Google Play.
