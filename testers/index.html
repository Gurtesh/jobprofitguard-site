Tester guide

JPG Test is the full Job Profit Guard app on a separate test server. Nothing you do in it touches real customers or real money, so try everything, and try to break it.

Installing it

  1. Tap Download JPG Test, then open the file when it finishes.
  2. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect says it doesn't recognise the app, tap More details → Install anyway.

It shows up as JPG Test with a blue icon. The real Job Profit Guard app, if you have it, stays installed.

Your own account

Every tester gets their own account, just like a real customer would. It's free on the test server, and every feature is switched on.

Boss

Run your own business

Open JPG Test, tap Create owner account (or Sign up with Google), and choose Me and my crew. Set up your business, then add a job, from scratch or from a quote.

Crew

Join someone's crew

The boss goes to Account → My crew and taps Create invite code. The crew tester makes an account, chooses I'm on a crew, and enters the code. Pair up with another tester, or use a second phone.

Use made-up customers and numbers. This is a test server, and we may clear it between test rounds. No sign-in code is emailed in JPG Test: your email and password are enough.

Just want to look around first? Ask for the sample business login: a shared demo with five jobs already in progress.

Known issues

  • Play Protect may warn about the app when you install it. That's normal outside Google Play.
  • If the camera or photo picker won't open, close the app completely and open it again.
  • Voice recordings stay on the phone that made them. The words are saved, but the audio is gone if you uninstall.
  • Frank, and the voice and receipt reading, can take several seconds.

Found something? Report it

In the app, go to Account → Report a problem. Crew: the Account tab. Tell us:

  1. what you were doing,
  2. what you expected, and
  3. what happened instead.

Add a screenshot if you can. Your phone, app version and last few screens are added for you. If the app won't open at all, email support@jobprofitguard.app.
